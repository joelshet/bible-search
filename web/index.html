    On a phone

    Searching

    "exact words"
    only verses with that phrase
    in:john in:nt
    narrow to a book or a group: ot, nt, law, history, poetry, prophets, gospels, paul, epistles
    -word
    leave out verses with that word
    G26 H430 agape
    every verse with that Greek or Hebrew word, however the KJV translated it
    ps 23 1 cor 13:4-7
    go straight to a passage

    On a result

    context
    the verses just before and after, opened in place; more widens it
    read
    start reading there; going back returns you to the result
    a word or a note
    the Hebrew or Greek word behind it

    Listening

    listen
    your device's voice reads on from the current verse, and the page follows
    a verse
    tap one while listening to jump there
